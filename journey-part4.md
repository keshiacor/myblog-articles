# The Wins: “Stella Got Her Groove Back” 💃🏽

### What I Learned After 18 Months of Getting Back to Coding While Working Full Time (Part 4)

It’s been officially a little over 18 months of coding, learning, juggling, doubting, restarting, and rediscovering. This is Part 4 of my series and we’re talking about wins🎉. Missed Part 1, Part 2 or Part 3 of this Summer series, no worries, I’ve linked them here for you. Catch up when you’re ready!

## Yes this ‘Stella’ Got Her Groove Back 💃🏽

Let’s talk confidence.
If you know where this title comes from, kudos to you. If not, let me catch you up: Stella is this iconic, beautiful woman of color who hasn’t been so lucky in love. She takes a spontaneous trip to Jamaica with her best friend Delilah (played by Whoopi Goldberg) and ends up meeting a much younger, very fine Jamaican man. What starts off as a fling becomes something deeper — a spark she didn’t expect but so badly needed.

Well… I may not have landed a forever love (yet 😏), but boy have I gotten my groove back.

After 18 months of juggling it all — relearning, rediscovering, making mistakes, living abroad, and laughing along the way — I’ve rebuilt my confidence to get back into the world of tech as a software engineer.

I never really left tech, but now I’ve made the comeback knowing I’ve got what it takes. I’m not saying I’m the most brilliant person in the room — but I am someone with the right mix of technical and soft skills.

I ask questions (lots of them — and good ones too!). I care about details. I’m humble enough to receive feedback without taking it personally. If it helps me become a better SWE, bring it, friend 💪🏽.

At first, the longer I was away, the harder it felt to switch back. And let’s not even talk about age — that thing needs to go into permanent retirement. We’re ignoring her 😅.

Time away can give perspective, but too much time away? It messes with your confidence more than your skills. Skills can be refreshed — it’s like riding a bike. But confidence? That takes work.

Imposter syndrome is a loud neighbor, and since that neighbor lives inside us… whew, it gets NOISY. But I’ve learned to talk back to it. To remind it who I am: A strong, curious, humble dev ready to grow. This journey isn’t for the faint of heart — and I’m still here.

## Caught Up & Current 🔄💻

Another big win? I’m officially up to speed with today’s tech stack.
I even completed Codecademy’s Full-Stack Engineer path as part of my relearning process, a full refresh from front to back with a structured curriculum. Learning React, digging into the backend, practicing real-world scenarios… all of it gave me the solid foundation I needed to feel current again. Didn’t I tell you losing confidence could make you jump through hoops?! 😅

Not just scraping by, but thriving. That was the goal. ✅

## Back to Passion Projects 🎨✈️

One of the best feelings has been creating again just for fun.
Not for a deadline. Not for a client. Just for me.

I’ve got a project in progress right now that was inspired by my time living and traveling in Barcelona last year 🇪🇸 something personal, meaningful, and technically challenging in the best way for me.

Reconnecting with that spark has reminded me why I fell in love with building things in the first place. There’s nothing like that “I just made this!” rush. Sidenote, that feeling always brings memories to my first ever Java programming class printing ‘Hello world’ in my first year of coding circa 2008 🥹.

## Mentorship & Encouragement Along the Way 💬🌱

This year, I’ve been lucky to cross paths with some truly generous and inspiring folks.

Whether it was on ADPList or in coffee chats with kind strangers-turned-peers, these conversations gave me perspective, validation, and often the exact encouragement I needed at the right moment. Special shout out to Lara! 🎉

I’ve had a few wonderful mentors — two of them recurring — who carved out time to guide me, share their stories, and help me reflect on mine. There was even a senior dev at work who offered to mentor me (life had other plans, unfortunately, but that’s a whole other story maybe for another day).

And then there are the quiet mentors, folks I follow online, watch speak at conferences, listen to their podcast weekly, or read blog posts from whose transparency and vulnerability about their own growth reminds me that I’m not alone in this.

Those small moments of support? They’ve been huge wins in their own way. Because we rise faster when someone’s willing to walk a few steps with us.

## Finding My People 🌍👯‍♀️

This journey hasn’t been a total solo one — and that’s another win worth celebrating.

I’ve started connecting more with the local dev community in Montréal, especially around JavaScript and React. Showing up to meetups, listening, sharing, laughing, it’s been a joy to learn alongside others who are just as passionate (and sometimes just as confused 😂).

Turns out, community is a superpower!
We were never meant to do this alone. And now? I’m starting to feel like I truly belong here. I BELONG HERE!

## So What’s Next For Me? 🚀

Right now, I’m looking for the right ‘first’ comeback full-time role as a software engineer where I can continue to grow, learn, build, and contribute meaningfully.\*

Good market or not — I’m not waiting around for perfect conditions.
This has been a long time coming, and I’m stepping into this next chapter with hunger, grit, humility, and excitement!

I’m walking into the future with a heart full of code and full of gratitude to those who cheer me on and take time to invest in me, a playlist full of salsa, and a Notion page full of side project ideas that don’t involve another Todo list app. Let’s go. 🙌🏽💻✨
